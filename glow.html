The short version: Glow stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Your skincare journey stays private — always.

📋 Information We Collect

Glow is designed with privacy first. All data you enter is stored exclusively on your device using on-device storage (Hive). This includes:

  • Your name (entered during onboarding)
  • Your skincare goals and preferences
  • Your daily routine steps and products
  • Your progress and check-in history
  • Notification preferences and reminder times

None of this information ever leaves your device.

🚫 What We Do Not Collect

Glow does not collect, transmit, or process any of the following:

✕ Name or email address
✕ Location data
✕ Device identifiers
✕ Analytics or usage data
✕ Crash reports (sent externally)
✕ Photos or camera data
✕ Health data
✕ Payment information

📱 Local Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using Hive, a lightweight on-device database. This data:

  • Is never uploaded to any server
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Remains entirely under your control
  • Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app

🔔 Notifications

Glow may request permission to send you local push notifications to remind you of your skincare routine. These reminders are:

  • Scheduled entirely on-device — no server is involved
  • Only sent if you grant notification permission
  • Fully configurable and can be disabled at any time in your device settings

We do not use notifications to deliver advertising or tracking content.

🏪 App Store & Third-Party Services

Glow is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect certain standard device and usage data as part of their platform services, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Glow also uses the native In-App Review API (provided by Apple/Google) to ask for your feedback. This interaction is handled entirely by the operating system — we do not collect or store your review or rating.

👧 Children's Privacy

Glow is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since all data is stored locally and we collect no personal information, the app is designed to be safe for all ages. If you have concerns, please contact us.

🛡️ Data Security

Because all your data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to external servers, the security of your data is inherently protected by your device's built-in security mechanisms (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, and iOS/Android data encryption).

⚖️ Your Rights & Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • View your data: All data is accessible within the app
  • Delete your data: Uninstall the app to permanently delete all stored data
  • Modify your data: Edit or reset your profile and routines at any time from within the app
