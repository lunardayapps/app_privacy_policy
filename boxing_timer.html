Executive Summary & Architecture

This Privacy Policy explains how Boxing Timer ("Combat Clock", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application across iOS and Android devices.

Unlike typical fitness apps that upload training regimens, health metrics, and biometric profiles to remote commercial cloud servers, Combat Clock operates entirely offline. We do not own, operate, or maintain any backend servers that receive, collect, or store your personal information.

🛡️

Under Apple App Store Nutrition Labels and Google Play Data Safety standards:
Combat Clock qualifies for "Data Not Collected". We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or monetize personal data in any form.

02

Information We Do NOT Collect

To provide complete transparency, our application has deliberately been built without the capability to access, harvest, or transmit any of the following categories of data:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No real names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
  • Location Data: No GPS, Wi-Fi location, IP geolocation, or background location tracking.
  • Biometric Data: No facial recognition, fingerprint data, or camera sensor biometric scans.
  • Financial or Payment Data: No credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.
  • Device Identifiers & Tracking: No Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), Google Advertising ID (AAID), or persistent cross-app tracking fingerprints.
  • Network Traffic: No remote server calls. The application contains zero HTTP/REST API endpoints.

Device Permissions & Hardware Sensor Usage

Boxing and combat sports present unique physical demands: athletes wear 16oz boxing gloves, wraps, and sweat. Combat Clock leverages native device hardware features to provide hands-free control, acoustic penetration, and visual alert signals. Here is a comprehensive breakdown of every permission and sensor used:

Permission / Sensor Platform Type Technical Function & Privacy Guarantee
Hardware Proximity Sensor iOS / Android Hardware Sensor Feature: Wave-to-Pause (Hands-Free Control)
Uses the device's front infrared proximity sensor (UIDevice.isProximityMonitoringEnabled). Allows athletes wearing boxing gloves to wave their hand near the top speaker to pause/resume rounds without touching the screen.
Guarantee: Reads only a binary proximity state (Near / Far). No camera feeds, facial imagery, or biometric measurements are accessed or captured.
LED Camera Flashlight / Torch iOS / Android Hardware Control Feature: Visual Strobe Flash Alert
Activates the rear camera LED torch via platform torch mode (AVCaptureDevice.torchMode) to flash 3 times at round start, 10s warning, and round end.
Guarantee: Used strictly as a visual flashlight strobe for deaf, hard-of-hearing, or headphone-wearing fighters. Combat Clock NEVER accesses the camera lens, photos, or video stream.
Background Audio Playback iOS: audio Background Mode
Android: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 System Service Feature: Background Bell & Voice Coach Audio
Ensures round bells, countdown beeps, and combination callouts continue playing seamlessly when your screen locks or when multitasking.
Guarantee: Only plays pre-bundled local audio assets and synthesizes local speech. Does not record or monitor microphone audio.

05

Third-Party Services & Operating System APIs

Combat Clock minimizes dependencies on external code. Here are the only platform-provided frameworks integrated into the application:

  • Apple StoreKit & Google Play In-App Review: We utilize official operating system dialogs (SKStoreReviewController / Google Play In-App Review API) to allow satisfied users to rate the app. This interaction is managed directly by your device's operating system. We receive zero identifying information regarding your review.
  • On-Device Text-To-Speech (TTS): The Virtual Coach combination announcer utilizes your device's built-in local voice synthesizer (iOS AVSpeechSynthesizer / Android TTS Engine). Your workout routines and punch cues are never sent to cloud speech APIs.

No Advertising Networks: Combat Clock does not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or any third-party ad networks. You will never be tracked for targeted advertisements.

06

Data Retention, Control & Deletion Rights

Because all application data is stored strictly in your local device sandbox:

  • You Have Full Control: You can edit or delete any custom workout preset directly from the Routine Builder at any time.
  • Instant Complete Deletion: If you uninstall or delete Combat Clock from your iOS or Android device, all associated stored preferences, custom routines, onboarding answers, and workout history logs are permanently and irreversibly purged by your device's operating system.
  • System Storage Reset: You can also instantly wipe all stored application data without uninstalling by clearing app data in your device settings (Android: Settings > Apps > Combat Clock > Storage > Clear Data; iOS: Delete and reinstall).

International Compliance (GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

European Union & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR):
Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, port, and erase your personal data. Because we do not store, collect, or transmit your personal data to any server, your personal data is already entirely under your exclusive custody on your personal device.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA):
The California Consumer Privacy Act requires disclosures about personal information collection and sales. Combat Clock does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising, nor have we done so in the preceding 12 months.
