Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Grip, a Study Management Application. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our services.

Information Collection

Grip is designed to respect your privacy. As such:

  • Local Storage: All your personal inputs—such as your name, daily targets, weekly goals, subjects, tasks, and study session logs—are stored entirely on your device's local database. We do not upload this data to any remote servers.
  • No Personal Account: You do not need to register an account or provide an email address, phone number, or social media profile to use Grip.

Analytics & Diagnostics

To improve our service and understand how users interact with Grip, we collect anonymous usage statistics:

  • We use Mixpanel to track anonymized interactions (such as starting a timer, setting a weekly target, or creating a subject).
  • This diagnostic data does not contain any personally identifiable information and is used exclusively for improving app stability and performance.

Third-Party Services

Apart from anonymous analytics, we do not share, sell, or distribute your information to third parties. We do not serve advertisements in Grip, so your study habits are never shared with advertising networks.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you have absolute control over your information:

  • You can reset or delete your local database and preferences directly from the app's Settings menu.
  • Uninstalling the Grip application from your device will permanently delete all local databases, session history, and preferences.

Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is requested or transmitted, we remain fully compliant with child safety protection guidelines.

Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
