Privacy Policy

Pregnancy Safe — Last updated: May 8, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Pregnancy Safe. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Pregnancy Safe mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information with care, especially given the sensitive nature of pregnancy-related health data.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at tithipal699@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Information you provide during onboarding

  • Your first name (optional)
  • Your age
  • Your pregnancy status (expecting, breastfeeding, or planning)
  • Your trimester (if applicable)
  • Whether this is your first pregnancy
  • Your focus areas and dietary preferences

This information is stored locally on your device using secure device storage. It is not linked to any personally identifiable account and is not transmitted to our servers in a form that identifies you personally.

Scan and chat activity

  • Barcodes you scan are sent to third-party product databases to retrieve ingredient data.
  • Ingredient lists and your profile context are sent to our AI analysis backend to generate safety results.
  • Messages you send to Mama AI are processed by our backend to generate responses. These messages are not permanently stored by us.

Anonymous authentication data

We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to identify your app session. No email address, phone number, or account credentials are required or collected. An anonymous user ID is generated automatically by Firebase and is used solely for security purposes such as rate limiting.

Usage data

We may collect non-identifiable usage data such as app crashes and error logs to improve performance. This data does not include your health profile or message content.

How We Use Your Information

  • To personalise safety analysis results based on your pregnancy stage and preferences.
  • To provide AI-powered responses through the Mama AI chat feature.
  • To look up product ingredient data from third-party databases.
  • To prevent abuse of our backend services using anonymous session identifiers.
  • To improve App performance and fix technical issues.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or marketing companies.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle data.

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Auth, Firebase App Check) — Used for anonymous authentication and to protect our backend API from abuse. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts — Open-source product databases used to retrieve ingredient lists from scanned barcodes. Product barcodes are sent to these services over HTTPS. Open Food Facts Privacy
  • Go-UPC — A supplementary product lookup service used to find product data when other sources do not return results.
  • Cloudflare Workers (our AI backend) — Our AI safety analysis and Mama AI chat run on a backend hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Requests are authenticated using Firebase tokens and processed without permanently storing your message content. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Data Storage and Retention

Your onboarding profile, scan history, and saved products are stored locally on your device using device-level storage. This data is not uploaded to our servers and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

We do not maintain a server-side user database. Your anonymous Firebase session ID may be retained by Firebase in accordance with their data retention policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at tithipal699@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personally identifiable information, most traditional data rights requests do not apply. However, you can:

  • Delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.
  • Contact us to request deletion of any Firebase anonymous session data associated with your session.

If you are located in the European Union or California, and believe additional rights apply to your situation, please reach out to us at tithipal699@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. All communication between the App and our backend is encrypted using HTTPS. Our AI backend requires a valid Firebase App Check token on every request to prevent unauthorised access.

No security system is completely impenetrable. While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

Medical Disclaimer

Pregnancy Safe is an informational tool only. The safety analyses and AI responses provided by the App are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your doctor, obstetrician, or midwife before making any health-related decisions during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
